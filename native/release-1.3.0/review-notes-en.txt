Version 1.3.0 adds one offline feature: Settings (設定・保存) > 作品を保存・読み込み > 「編み図をPDFで書き出す（印刷用）」. The user picks paper (A4, B5, A3, Letter), orientation, colour / colour + symbols / black-and-white symbols, and cell size; page 1 is previewed. 「PDFを作成」 renders the chart pages on the device and opens the standard share sheet (Print, Save to Files, etc.). Nothing is uploaded; no network is used. It works while the chart is locked and after the trial, because it only reads the project (viewing and export stay available after the trial, as before).

Version 1.2.2 adds one offline option: Settings (設定・保存) > 新しくつくる > 「文字から編み図をつくる」 now has a 書体 (typeface) menu with 7 choices: the system gothic plus six bundled fonts (Misaki 8x8 and PixelMplus12 pixel fonts, Zen Maru Gothic, Dela Gothic One, Yusei Magic, Pacifico). Pixel fonts render one dot as one stitch (scaled by whole stitches). The stitch and row counts now follow the entered text and typeface so letters are not squeezed; typing a size keeps it. The fonts are bundled in the app (no download, no network) and are all licensed for commercial use and redistribution (SIL Open Font License 1.1, M+ FONT LICENSE, Misaki font licence); the licences and credits are linked from the in-app help page (使い方).

Version 1.2.1 adds:
1. Settings (設定・保存) > 新しくつくる > 「丸ヨークの編み図をつくる」: enter gauge and pick a size (XS-5XL, standard or relaxed fit) or type chest, upper arm, neck and yoke depth. The app calculates the neck cast-on, number of repeats, yoke rows, increase rows, the finished yoke count and the body/sleeve split, and creates one repeat of a top-down yoke chart with mirrored edge increases (M1R/M1L) and row notes. Fully local; no network.
2. First launch: a sample Nordic chart opens and a skippable three-step guide follows the trial sheet. Not shown to users with a saved project or a purchased/legacy unlock.
3. During the trial a small 「無料体験 残り○日」 label is shown; it opens the purchase sheet.
4. The standard StoreKit review request (AppStore.requestReview) is called at most once, after 20 rows have been completed in total, never at launch, around purchase sheets or after errors. No custom rating prompt.
The first-launch trial sheet (since 1.2.0) states the 7-day duration, what stops afterwards, the JPY 3000 one-time unlock and that there is no automatic charge before the trial starts.

Version 1.2.0 adds one offline editing feature: Settings (設定・保存) > 新しくつくる > 「写真から2〜3色の編み図をつくる」.
Choose any photo. The app converts it locally into a 2- or 3-yarn knitting chart: edge-preserving smoothing, multi-level Otsu fills, and difference-of-Gaussians line art for eyes, nose, mouth and outlines, plus speck cleanup. 「写真を切り抜く」 opens a crop view (drag / pinch). The original photo and the resulting chart are shown side by side while the user adjusts colors, size, line strength, contrast, brightness balance and cleanup. 「この編み図をつくる」 replaces the current project; the chart then remains fully editable.
No AI service, network request or server is used; the photo never leaves the device. Photo access is user-initiated through the standard file/photo picker.
Trial notices: on first launch (before the trial starts) a sheet states the 7-day duration, that editing and the row counter stop afterwards while viewing/export remain, and the JPY 3000 one-time unlock with no automatic charge; 「7日間無料で始める」 starts the JPY 0 trial IAP. One reminder appears in the last 24 hours and one notice when the trial ends; each offers the unlock and 「あとで」.
The business model is unchanged from 1.1.0: free download + non-consumable 7-day trial (jp.amimononote.ios.trial7, JPY 0) + non-consumable permanent unlock (jp.amimononote.ios.lifetime, JPY 3000). No subscription, login or external payment.
