Version 1.2.0 adds one offline editing feature: Settings (設定・保存) > 新しくつくる > 「写真から2〜3色の編み図をつくる」.
Choose any photo. The app converts it locally into a 2- or 3-yarn knitting chart based on brightness (automatic levels, multi-level Otsu thresholds, optional local contrast and speck cleanup). The original photo and the resulting chart are shown side by side while the user adjusts colors, size, crop zoom/position, brightness balance, detail emphasis and cleanup. 「この編み図をつくる」 replaces the current project; the chart then remains fully editable.
No AI service, network request or server is used; the photo never leaves the device. Photo access is user-initiated through the standard file/photo picker.
The business model is unchanged from 1.1.0: free download + non-consumable 7-day trial (jp.amimononote.ios.trial7, JPY 0) + non-consumable permanent unlock (jp.amimononote.ios.lifetime, JPY 3000). No subscription, login or external payment.
